Questions

Good to know

Does my data leave my Mac?

No. Usage history stays in a file on your Mac, and the AI runs on your Mac. The only things Bondi sends are the license check and the update check.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. The AI uses Apple's on-device model where Apple Intelligence is available; other Macs can download a free, small open model from Settings.

Will it slow my Mac down?

No. With its windows closed Bondi uses about 0.3% of one CPU core. It only samples faster while you're looking at it.

Can Bondi quit apps by itself?

Never. Bondi suggests one fix at a time and asks before it quits, stops or unloads anything.

What happens after the trial?

The menu bar keeps showing your CPU for free. Everything else waits for a license.
