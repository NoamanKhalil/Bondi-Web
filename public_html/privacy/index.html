Privacy policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

Bondi is made by Jabble Super Intelligence Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Jabble", "we"). Bondi is built so that what you do on your Mac stays on your Mac. This policy says exactly what leaves it, and what our website and our payment partner see.

What stays on your Mac

Everything Bondi measures (apps, processes, memory, CPU, battery, disk, network, sensors) and your 30 days of history stay in files on your Mac. We never receive them. There's no account and no analytics.

On-device AI

Bondi's AI features run on your Mac: Apple's on-device model where Apple Intelligence is available, or a small open model you choose to download. Your questions, your history and the AI's answers are never uploaded. If you press Download for the open model in Settings, your Mac downloads it from its publisher (currently Qwen's official page on Hugging Face), which sees a normal download request from your internet address.

What Bondi sends

WhenWhat is sentWhy
Starting and checking the free trialAn anonymous device ID (a one-way code made on your Mac, not your Mac's serial number) and Bondi's versionSo the 7-day trial can't restart on reinstall
Activating or checking a license (about once a day)Your license key or activation token, the anonymous device ID, and your Mac's nameTo keep one license on one Mac, and show you which Mac holds it
Checking for updatesBondi's versionTo offer new versions

Our server also receives your internet address with each request, as every web server does. We use it only to prevent abuse (for example, too many activation attempts) and don't keep it with your license.

Buying Bondi

Paddle.com is the merchant of record for all purchases: Paddle sells Bondi to you, processes your payment and handles tax and receipts, under Paddle's privacy policy. We never see your card details. Paddle tells us your email address and the order details, which we keep to issue your license, email you your key, and help you if you lose it.

Our website

trybondi.app has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. If you pick light or dark mode, your browser remembers that choice on your device; it's never sent to us. Our host keeps standard server logs for security. The checkout on our buy page is Paddle's.

Signing up for updates

If you sign up for updates on trybondi.app, we keep your name, your email address, the internet (IP) address you signed up from, and your country as a two-letter code. We work out the country on our own server, from your IP address using a list of which networks are registered in which country (or, if the address isn't on it, from your browser's time zone); we don't send your IP address to any outside lookup service. We use these only to email you about Bondi's beta and launch, to see roughly where interest comes from, and to stop abuse of the form. We don't sell or share them. Every email we send lets you stop further emails, and you can ask us to delete your sign-up at any time.

How long we keep things

License and order records: for as long as the license exists, and afterwards as long as tax and accounting law requires. Trial records (the anonymous device ID and start date): up to 2 years. Emails we sent you: 2 years. Update sign-ups: until you ask us to remove them, or until we stop sending updates.

Your choices

Deleting Bondi's history is one click in Settings, Data. To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or to object to how we use it, email support@trybondi.app. We answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have further rights under local law, and we honour them.

Children

Bondi isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so on this page and in Bondi's release notes. Questions: support@trybondi.app, Jabble Super Intelligence Inc., [company mailing address].
