bird is the process that syncs iCloud Drive on your Mac, together with cloudd. Despite the odd name it's part of macOS and safe; when it's busy, it's uploading or downloading many files, or catching up right after you sign in to iCloud.

## Key points

- bird is iCloud Drive syncing, working together with cloudd.
- It gets busy uploading or downloading many files, or right after signing in to iCloud.
- Stopping it doesn't help for long: macOS starts it again when it's needed.

## What bird does

When you save a file to iCloud Drive, or another device changes one, bird and cloudd move it between your Mac and iCloud. You can see what's still syncing in Finder: files waiting to upload or download show a status icon next to their name.

## Why bird is using CPU

Bondi's note: *Uploading or downloading many files, or right after signing in to iCloud.*

If it stays busy for a long time, look at what else is happening: a big download, an import, an update or a sync is usually the reason, and it settles once that finishes. If your whole Mac feels slow, [Why is my Mac slow?](/blog/why-is-my-mac-slow/) walks through the other usual causes.

Stopping it doesn't help for long: macOS starts it again when it's needed.
